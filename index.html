Pelicula

Rank every film you've ever seen.

Pelicula is a film ranking and discovery app for iOS. Build your personal ranked list of every movie and TV show you've watched, discover what to watch next, and see how your taste compares with friends.

What Pelicula does

  • Rank films and TV shows on a personal tier list
  • Track everything you've seen and want to see
  • Get personalized recommendations based on your rankings
  • Compare taste and rankings with friends
  • Compete on a global leaderboard

How we use your data

When you sign in with Google, Pelicula receives your name and email address. We use this to create and identify your account.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than providing the Pelicula service. Your rankings and watchlist are stored securely and are only visible to you and users you choose to follow.

For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
